WHY ASIBITIN HAKORI?

Asibitin Hakori is designed to make it easier for patients to discover dental clinics, contact clinics directly and request dental appointments.

  • ✓ Easy clinic discovery
  • ✓ Direct WhatsApp contact
  • ✓ Phone contact
  • ✓ Location and directions
  • ✓ Clinic information
  • ✓ Appointment requests
  • ✓ Verification system

Powered by Imamgee Dental Care. This does not mean every listed clinic is owned or operated by Imamgee Dental Care.

Clear, precise clinic status

Clinic listings can be pending, verified, rejected or suspended. The platform does not describe an unverified clinic as trusted or verified.

Clinic opening status is calculated only when the supplied hours are sufficiently structured. Listings such as "open late" are not treated as exact 24-hour schedules.

Privacy Policy

This prototype stores clinic, registration and appointment records in your browser's localStorage. Patient information should only be collected when necessary. A production platform must use secure server-side storage, access controls, encryption and appropriate data-retention practices.

Terms of Use

Clinic information may change. Users should confirm clinic availability, hours and services directly with the clinic. The prototype does not guarantee treatment availability or outcomes.

Healthcare Disclaimer

Asibitin Hakori is a discovery and contact platform. It does not replace professional medical or dental assessment. For serious symptoms, seek appropriate urgent medical care.
